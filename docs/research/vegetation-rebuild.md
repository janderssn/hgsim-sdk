# Vegetation references for the alpine rebuild

Inspected 2026-11-08. These are primary-source implementation references, not a claim that the game matches their demos. No source code or binary asset from these repositories was copied by this research pass. The vegetation implementation is original; retaining this distinction avoids importing example-specific renderer state, physics or unrelated assets.

## Asset candidates and provenance constraints

The Fluffy Tree repository contains an original scene GLB at `assets/landscape-glb.glb` under its repository MIT licence. It was inspected as a scene/code reference; its GLB and separate HDRI were **not** imported. A scene-wide asset would need extraction, scale/material review and licence attribution before shipping. The visible style is useful, but wholesale replacement would discard this game's flight corridor and matching collision world.

[Kenney Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit) and [Quaternius Ultimate Stylized Nature](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatestylizednature.html) are relevant asset leads. Requests to both primary pages were rejected by the environment proxy with HTTP 403, so their current downloads/licence files could not be inspected in this pass. Do not infer licence verification from their reputation. The [third-party Godot conversion](https://github.com/walterpalladino/godot-quaternius-ultimate-stylized-nature) has an MIT repository licence but did not provide a separately verified upstream asset grant in the inspected tree; this alone is insufficient evidence for importing its models. No assets from these sources were imported.

## Concrete visual priorities

1. Coverage: continuous overlapping low growth under a smaller number of taller curved grass fans, with retained open paths and short mown scoring field.
2. Light: broad warm blade tips, darker roots and smooth upward-biased normals; preserve cast shadow attenuation rather than adding unconditional emissive glow.
3. Species hierarchy: conifer mass with recognisable pale-trunk deciduous groves, warmer at the forest edge and less uniform height/spacing.
4. Distance: keep herb detail near the camera while a cheaper ground-coloured grass layer bridges the foreground into the tree line.
5. Evidence: assess actual game captures at the same meadow and flight views. A reference implementation or concept image is not evidence of runtime parity.
