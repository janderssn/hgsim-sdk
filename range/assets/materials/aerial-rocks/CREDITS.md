# Aerial Rocks 02

Photographic material **Aerial Rocks 02**, by **Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven**.
Original: <https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_rocks_02>.

The material is released under **CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain**, including
commercial use, modification and redistribution. Full terms are retained in
`LICENSE.txt`; Poly Haven's official license-page source is retained as
`polyhaven-license.json`.

The original three 2k PNG files were retrieved unchanged from the
[Godot Overgrown Subway mirror](https://github.com/mikatomik/Godot-4-Overgrown-Subway-Demo/tree/b3d0d0f8f13561aa9edc787a9fa0322939784ab6/subway/textures/cliff)
at commit `b3d0d0f8f13561aa9edc787a9fa0322939784ab6`. Their Git blob IDs and
SHA-256 digests were verified and are recorded in `manifest.json`. The mirror's
README is retained as `source-notice.txt`. Only the three named photographic
material maps are included here.

Independent attribution in the [PlayCanvas engine's terrain asset credits](https://github.com/playcanvas/engine/blob/4b305e9b9c2cac2397b65f3ef3915a84a7c7508e/examples/assets/textures/terrain/polyhaven.credits.txt)
identifies this material, its author, original URL and CC0 license. A copy is
retained in `provenance.txt`. Poly Haven's
[official license-page source](https://github.com/Poly-Haven/polyhaven.com/blob/b1a6aa13afc03ae00860f6ac5fe6d5daf8e95356/public/locales/en/license.json)
states that its textures are CC0 and can be modified and redistributed.

Runtime derivatives:

- `color.webp`: 2048 × 2048, WebP quality 90; use sRGB sampling.
- `normal.webp`: 1024 × 1024, lossless WebP. The original OpenGL normal vectors
  are area averaged and renormalized; the positive Y convention is retained.
  Use linear sampling.
- `roughness.webp`: 1024 × 1024, area averaged, lossless WebP. Use linear
  sampling of the red channel.

All source and derivative sizes, checksums and transformations are recorded in
`manifest.json`. The three runtime maps total 4,322,016 bytes.
