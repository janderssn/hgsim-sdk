Range

Each page flies short, repeatable scenarios on any wing, seen from the side, the top, behind and in front, with the numbers, the traces and the feel values that shape them. A scripted pilot flies each the same way every time, or you fly it.

  • LaunchThe pickup, the balance on the shoulders, the run and the lift-off: ramp, cliff and slope, calm, breezy, gusty and cross.
  • FlightPitch, speed, roll and the stall: a held dive, a release, reversals, turns, a spiral, straight and turning stalls.
  • LandingThe final and the flare: at the cue, early, late, none, fast, eased out; calm, windy and gusty.
